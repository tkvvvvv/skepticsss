Morality Argument for Atheism

Saturday, October 24, 2026 Rebuttal

Argument proposed by @faux.status.literatus and @rommelasianpromax

The argument “Atheism allows you to be a narcissist, misogynist, racist, homophobic, etc., without consequences.”

Justification

We do not have a problem with this argument in itself, given that atheism has no worldview of its own. However, we need to be clear about what atheism is. The universal definition of atheism is “a person who lacks, or does not hold, belief in a deity or god.” One thing theists get wrong is treating atheism as a belief system. It is an epistemic stance toward the proposition “God exists,” and it concerns belief only.

Rebuttal 1: The Philosophical Stance

Philosophically speaking, atheism is an epistemic stance toward the proposition that “God exists.” It is not an ethical stance. Atheism does not tell you what is right or wrong.

Rebuttal 2: Moral Subjectivity / Moral Relativism

The argument claims that we can be narcissistic, misogynistic, racist, homophobic, and so on, without consequences. Moral relativism is a good rebuttal here: if you are an atheist and you belong to a group where being a “narcissist” is not allowed, you will be shunned or punished.

Rebuttal 3: “No Consequences”

If we define atheism properly, it is true that it is not a systematic belief that prevents you from behaving this way. But theists miss the social consequences. Being a narcissist, misogynist, racist, or homophobe is not a mere preference or liking of an individual. So what are the consequences? They are social: people get angry at you and reject you, and because of your character they will hate you and avoid you.

Closing Statement

The argument has no real basis and shows a lack of understanding of what atheism means. It relies on subjective opinion instead of evidence, and it pushes the idea that “atheism is not a belief system,” or that “if you don't have God, you can't have consequences.” Note that the examples given in the argument are not morally related to atheism at all.
